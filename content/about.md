<p style="margin-top: 0;">
   I'm a photographer and naval architect based in New Hampshire. I've lived in New England my whole life.

<p>
    My primary work, Eastern Americana, explores the human history of the New England landscape. I grew up fascinated by old things, and enjoy visiting and photographing historic structures and spaces.
</p>

<p>
    I also enjoy marine photography - shooting working waterfronts, ships, boats, yachts, and whatever else I find.
</p>

<p>
    I work across multiple formats and enjoy darkroom and alt-process printing. In 2022, I founded <a href="https://www.instagram.com/newenglandanalog" target="_blank" style="color: var(--text-main); text-decoration: underline;">New England Analog</a>, a community for film photographers in the Northeast.
</p>
